YXNGJU V35 — ENEMY FLEET PROGRESSION

- Player hull reduced to 68; player ship sprite scaled down.
- Lives reduced to 2; respawn invulnerability reduced to 2.4 seconds.
- Enemy collision and projectile damage increased.
- Repair cells are rare (2.5% drop chance).
- Ten enemy ships cropped from the user-provided pixel-art reference are used as enemy sprites.
- Ten ordered stages: one enemy ship class per stage, three enemies active at once, 12 kills required per stage. Enemy HP and speed scale up each stage.
- Victory screen appears only after all ten stages are cleared.
- Browser/device playtesting not performed; test on GitHub Pages before replacing the live site.
